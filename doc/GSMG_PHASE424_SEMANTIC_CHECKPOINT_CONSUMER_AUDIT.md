# GSMG Phase 424 — Semantic Checkpoint Consumer Audit

## Result

**Outcome:** `recognition_only_no_forward_edge`

Phase 423 convergence strengthens the `BYE` / `CIAO BELLA O` farewell family
as the best recognition echo. It does not select a unique downstream artifact,
decoder, or consumer. No password variants were generated and no cipher oracle
was called.

No family passes all five pre-registered gates.

## Strongest family: farewell

`HYE -> BYE` is a controlled boundary result: the partial `a-i` mirror maps
the full endings rail to the only dictionary output in the fixed `BUT` control
family. The authenticated Phase-3.2.1 plaintext visibly ends `CIAO BELLA O`.
Community discussion independently connected that tail with goodbye/hello
duality and bye-to-beauty-O years before the HYE-to-BYE audit, so the family has
evidence beyond the shared Phase 423 prompt.

That is enough to make farewell the best recognition family, but not enough to
move forward:

- all bridge messages are community-authored;
- the complete creator `ciao` inventory contains ordinary sign-offs, not a
  selection of CIAO as yin-yang;
- no deterministic `BYE -> CIAO BELLA O` operation is specified; and
- no source says how the earlier plaintext tail consumes or selects FAED,
  DBBI, `thispassword`, SHA, or a blob.

The family identifies a semantic neighborhood, not a program edge.

## Why the other families stop earlier

`SALVATION` points back to the authenticated `SalPhaseIon` title, but the
replacement rebus is researcher-established and the title is the page
container rather than an output reached from the Architect boundary. Its only
remaining functional role is an untestable post-decryption checksum.

The Architect-rail family reproduces the current boundary. Existing controls
show that `BUT/HYE` lacks an independent discriminator proving it is the
promised yin-yang artifact, and no downstream operation survives.

The creator-macro family consists of exact prompt-visible text. Solver
agreement on those literals cannot resolve what `yinyang` or the in-front-of-
your-eyes phrase refers to.

`HALFANDBETTERHALF` and `LIVE` are backward extractions from the already-solved
Phase-3.2.2 answer. No source reselects either at the later boundary.

## Consumer audit

Six predeclared consumer roles were checked. None is selected by Phase 423:

- recognition output directly as `thispassword`: closed for literal forms;
- decoded FAED plaintext as `thispassword`: live, but decoder unknown;
- DBBI/FAED joint result as `thispassword`: live, but operator unknown;
- paired page textareas as yin-yang: not qualified;
- *Cosmic Duality* as the reached artifact: wrong boundary and no operation;
- `SalPhaseIon` title as the reached artifact: container, not a forward edge.

## Conclusion

Phase 423 added reproducibility to the farewell recognition model but did not
add the missing causal edge. The most local surviving dataflow remains:

```text
FAED
-> unknown decoder
-> decoded result
-> thispassword
-> adjacent SHA/SALPH region
```

The next admissible evidence must constrain a FAED decoder or an explicit
DBBI/FAED relationship. More variants of `BYE`, `CIAO`, `SALVATION`, the rails,
or macro literals are not licensed by this result.
