# Post-Phase-452 Scientific Experiment Portfolio

> [!caution] Incubation note
> This records possible future work. It does not authorize a search, create a
> finding, promote a candidate, or alter an Open Gap Registry closure
> condition. Every executable item needs a frozen protocol and stop rules.

## Purpose

Reconcile twelve proposed directions against completed work through Phase 452.
The project does not primarily lack compute: it lacks independently selected
operators, consumers, roles, and primary sources. Statistical neatness may
corroborate a construction, but cannot replace the closure conditions in
[GSMG_OPEN_GAP_REGISTRY](../GSMG_OPEN_GAP_REGISTRY.md).

## 12. Family-specific false-discovery calibration

> [!info] Executed as Phase 453
> The symbolic lane is complete: FF67 is unusual but unselected; KIT is
> null-sensitive; GGN and Roman are common under the familywise calibration.
> Dual-stream and artifact lanes remain separate future phases.

Build matched-null populations and measure how often the project's real
discovery procedures manufacture results at least as exact-looking as KIT,
FF67, ggn, or 401/400/73.

There is no universal null. Use separate frozen lanes:

- **symbolic transforms:** arithmetic, reversal, A1Z26, byte/hex, Roman
  projection, rail and prefix selection;
- **dual streams:** token, transition, topology, and role-split statistics for
  DBBI/FAED-like streams; and
- **artifact fingerprints:** formatting features calibrated against unrelated
  artifacts from the same ecosystem and era.

Implement the symbolic lane first. Freeze transforms, endpoint classes, null
generators, trial counts, random seeds, controls, and familywise rules before
scoring real data. Report complete ranks and ties. Adding a feature after
inspection requires a versioned protocol and fresh calibration.

Calibration changes evidentiary weight only. It cannot authenticate intent,
select a consumer, or close a gap. The detailed plan is
[Phase 453 — False-Discovery Calibration Harness Protocol](2026-08-29%20-%20Phase%20453%20False-Discovery%20Calibration%20Harness%20Protocol.md).

## 6. Recipient-side artifact acquisition

> [!info] Executed as Phase 454
> Full result: [Phase 454 Recipient-Side Artifact Acquisition](../GSMG_P454_RECIPIENT_ARTIFACT_ACQUISITION.md).
> The bounded local/GitHub/archive/discussion/search acquisition is complete.
> Two independent Stage-1 recipient copies strengthen known-byte provenance;
> no new primary artifact, clue content, or gap-closing evidence was recovered.
> Reopen only on a concrete new artifact identifier or digest.

Search for copies preserved by recipients rather than another copy on the
publisher's exhausted archive surfaces:

- browser caches and saved-page bundles;
- solver repository clones, abandoned branches, releases, and CI artifacts;
- Telegram forwarded-media originals rather than thumbnails;
- screenshots retaining URL bars, timestamps, or page state;
- archived ZIP/download directories;
- Reddit, forum, chat, or paste attachments;
- source maps, route manifests, robots files, and asset references inside
  third-party captures; and
- early solvers' local collections.

Every find is quarantined before clue analysis and must pass Phase 409's
fabrication/provenance discipline:

1. preserve original bytes and digest;
2. record acquisition path and retrieval time;
3. establish the earliest defensible chronology;
4. inspect claimant history and provenance continuity;
5. cross-check spam/fabrication address relationships where relevant;
6. classify creator-authored, creator-endorsed, community, or unattributed;
7. seek an independent copy or corroborating reference.

Semantic excitement cannot bypass provenance. This remains authorization-gated
external work.

## 9. Typed semantic checksum for thispassword

> [!info] Executed as Phase 455
> Full result: [Phase 455 Thispassword Typed Semantic Checksum](../GSMG_P455_THISPASSWORD_TYPED_SEMANTIC_CHECKSUM.md).
> All three roles survive the seven-axis typed constraint matrix with zero
> contradictions. No role or operand is selected; reopen only on new
> authenticated binding evidence or a genuinely new pre-registered type axis.

Extend Phases 373/376/377 with a fourth non-oracle test: treat authenticated
nearby words as type constraints rather than candidate values. Freeze the
three existing roles:

1. thispassword is the password for FAED;
2. FAED's answer is being labeled a password;
3. an Architect/FAED-adjacent result is the password for SALPH.

Map only authenticated terms—last words, password, sha256, enter, matrix, and
literal page order—to frozen constraints: input/output type, enterability,
hash state, result class, explicit cardinality/length, and object consumed.

No password generation, decryption, language score, or invented synonym sets.
Every constraint needs authenticated provenance or a solved-boundary positive
control. Freeze the role matrix before scoring. Return exactly
one_role_survives, multiple_roles_survive, all_roles_survive, or
constraint_system_inconsistent. A tie measures underdetermination.

G-MATPROD-001.

## 3. Closed-list two-role escape-pair experiment

Test whether FAED's preferred {g,i} and mirror-predicted {h,e} occupy different
roles. Freeze a small candidate list before output inspection:

- tokenizer versus coordinate/alphabet pair;
- raw-stream versus derived-stream escape pair;
- control-plane versus data-plane pair;
- omitted-symbol versus escape-symbol pair.

Each split needs a complete deterministic transform and one held-out prediction.
A model becomes review material only if both pairs are necessary, the transform
is unambiguous, it predicts an authenticated feature excluded from construction,
and it beats a pre-registered dual-stream null. Disposition remains
corroboration_only unless G-ESC-001's external closure condition is met.

## 1. Asymmetric DBBI/FAED generator tournament

Extend Phases 271–321 and 412/413 with a closed tournament:

- independent generators;
- shared latent alphabet with independent emissions;
- DBBI parameterizes FAED;
- FAED parameterizes DBBI;
- alternating control/data channels;
- one common source through two encoders.

Matched nulls preserve lengths, symbol counts, token-length distributions,
runs, autocorrelation, and authenticated boundaries. Score only frozen held-out
observables; no AES oracle or English-score promotion.

The protocol must declare any winner corroboration_only. It cannot override
G-YIN-001. BTCSEED is the standing control showing that a reproducible
asymmetric construction may properly remain unpromoted.

## 2. Formatting-level artifact authoring fingerprint

Extend Phase 410 beyond cryptographic construction to Base64 wrapping, final
newlines, HTML indentation/attribute order, line endings, container
serialization, PNG chunks/compression, SVG metadata, filename style,
bundler/minifier signatures, and provenance-safe timestamps.

Freeze the feature vector before clustering. Build contemporaneous unrelated
controls because generic defaults are not author fingerprints. Use solved
artifacts as positives only with authenticated authorship/chronology. Run the
artifact null alongside real clustering. Infer at most shared-toolchain or
shared-session compatibility, never shared puzzle operation.

## 8. Minimum-description-length formalization

Formalize the Scientific Theory Registry: credit authenticated facts explained;
penalize free parameters, analyst-selected transforms, unselected operands,
serialization and consumers, parked-gap dependencies, and post-result
exceptions. This compares existing explanations; it does not generate evidence.
Weights and sensitivity remain assumptions, so urgency is low.

## 5. Prospective chronology through Seed 11

Continue the passive new-evidence diff watch prospectively. Freeze predictions
and monitoring rules before future events. Do not choose retrospective cutoffs
inside the already-visible export: that is cutoff-shopping. This is Seed 11
continued, not a new backward-looking experiment.

## 4. Matrix-program induction — folded

The solved-boundary sample is too small for a flexible learned grammar; the DSL
and complexity penalty would dominate. Phase 341 already supplies the
defensible narrow method. Only fixed matrix multiplication survives as item
7's subtest.

## 10. Blinded instruction attachment — cut

Human panels conflict with the project's single-analyst convention. A parser
would duplicate Phases 376/377 absent a new authenticated linguistic feature.
The useful residue is absorbed by item 9's fourth typed-constraint test.

## 11. Original-environment archaeology — dropped

Browser, responsive-wrap, DOM, page-boundary, and encoding surfaces are already
heavily audited, including Phase 392. Broad replay creates excessive researcher
degrees of freedom. Reconsider only if new evidence selects a specific
environment or a recovered artifact demonstrably depends on one.
