## Capture protocol

Preserve original, uncropped, unadjusted files. Do not flatten or force the
binding merely to obtain a scan; use photographs if scanning risks damage or
hides the gatefold structure.

Capture in this order:

1. Front cover, back cover, spine, title page, copyright/edition page, and any
   identifiers.
2. Closed page sequence around the target (at minimum pages 55–60), showing
   pagination and how the gatefold sits in the binding.
3. Pages 57–58 before unfolding, straight-on and with all edges visible.
4. Each unfolding step, so the physical transformation and panel order can be
   reconstructed.
5. The completely opened gatefold as one uncropped overview.
6. Straight-on overlapping detail images sufficient to read every mark.
7. Reverse sides, folds, seams, edge printing, translucent/overlapping
   elements, and anything concealed in the closed state.
8. A scale/ruler and neutral gray or color reference in at least one additional
   frame, if available; also retain a clean frame without them.

Do not discard apparent blanks or duplicate views. Do not apply OCR, rotation,
contrast, sharpening, color correction, or recompression to the originals.
Derived working copies may be created later and must be labeled as derivatives.

## Pre-registered verdicts

- **Complete operation evidence:** all seven fields are `fixed`, with zero
  surviving alternatives, before inspecting any computed output.
- **Partial evidence:** at least one field is genuinely fixed but at least one
  remains absent or ambiguous. Record the reduction without filling the rest
  from theme or prior preferred models.
- **Relevant negative:** the complete target artifact is authenticated and
  legibly captured, but it fixes none of the seven fields.
- **Inconclusive capture:** authenticity, completeness, fold structure, or
  legibility is insufficient to classify one or more fields.

Obtaining and reviewing the pages can close the current acquisition action in
the gap registry without proving the proposed 31-character consumer. Promotion
of an operation still requires the worksheet's G1–G5 gates.
