of a fixed-term hit; chronological adjacency does not count.

## Evidence classes and decision rule

Each representation receives one of these classes:

- `creator_delivered`: bytes present in authenticated creator puzzle material;
- `clue_selected_derived`: exact intermediate selected by the documented solve
  chain, but not independently creator-published as a downstream operand;
- `decoded_output`: authenticated/reproducible solve output containing the
  instruction under examination.

Promotion to a Phase 437 `locally_selected` and `unique_representation` pass
requires creator-primary evidence that identifies one representation as the
later operand. A community walkthrough, community discussion, deterministic
derivability, or successful use as an intermediate decoder is insufficient.

Allowed dispositions:

- `downstream_representation_bound`: creator-primary evidence uniquely binds one;
- `workflow_privilege_without_downstream_binding`: the solve workflow privileges
  roles, but no representation is bound to `SOURCE CODES` downstream;
- `no_reproducible_workflow_privilege`: even the solve-role ordering is not reproducible.

## Stop conditions

Phase 438 generates no selected strings, prime rails, passwords, hashes of
candidate passwords, oracle calls, or GPU work. If no downstream binding is
found, Phase 437 remains gated. Any later extraction requires a separate frozen
protocol fixing source, unit, index base, direction, rail, boundary, and consumer.
