# Phase 424 — Semantic Checkpoint Consumer Audit Pre-Registration

## Question

Does Phase 423's exact convergence add enough independent evidence to promote
one semantic recognition family to a unique, authenticated downstream artifact
or executable consumer edge?

This is a provenance and dataflow audit. It does not generate password
variants, invoke a cipher oracle, infer correctness from AES output, or reopen
any exact candidate that Phase 423 already tested.

## Frozen input

The audit consumes only committed artifacts and existing assertion-backed
reconstructions:

- `phase423_result.json` and `phase423_execution_replay.py`;
- `yinyang_artifact_inventory_audit.py`;
- `post_yinyang_dataflow_audit.py`;
- the authenticated page order and creator-clue ledger; and
- prior BYE/CIAO and SalPhaseIon/SALVATION provenance audits.

The exact Phase 423 result must remain:

- five eligible submissions;
- 18 distinct candidates;
- 12 promotions;
- five comparator duplicates and seven comparison-new promotions;
- seven negative evaluations; and
- no terminal or structural hit.

## Frozen semantic families

Every promoted Phase 423 candidate belongs to exactly one of these five
predeclared families:

1. **farewell:** `BYE`, `CIAOBELLAO`;
2. **title salvation:** `SALVATION`, `SALVATIONpromised`;
3. **Architect rails:** `BUTHYE`, `BOTHULTIMATELYTHE`;
4. **creator macro literals:** `promised`,
   `verylaststepisatruegiveaway`,
   `itsinfrontofyoureyesbutyourenotseeingit`, `yinyang`; and
5. **solved-tail extraction:** `HALFANDBETTERHALF`, `LIVE`.

No singleton, non-promoted proposal, spelling variant, synonym, anagram, hash,
or concatenation outside this exact promoted set enters Phase 424.

## Promotion gates

A semantic family may identify a downstream consumer only if all five gates
are true:

1. **Source grounded:** its relevant text or artifact is authenticated, with
   community/researcher operations labeled separately.
2. **Correct boundary:** the family occurs at or after
   `lastwordsbeforearchichoice`, rather than merely echoing an earlier solved
   output or page-wide theme.
3. **Independent discriminator:** evidence beyond the shared Phase 423 prompt
   distinguishes this family from the other prompt-salient families.
4. **Unique referent:** the family selects exactly one already-authenticated
   artifact or state.
5. **Executable consumer:** a source-grounded deterministic edge specifies how
   that referent is consumed next.

Votes and ranks are descriptive only. Same-provider convergence on a shared
prompt cannot satisfy gate 3 by itself. A negative password evaluation cannot
be inverted into evidence for a non-password role.

## Frozen consumer universe

The only eligible downstream roles are those already present in the page and
dataflow audits:

- the recognition output itself is `thispassword`;
- decoded FAED plaintext is `thispassword`;
- a DBBI/FAED joint result is `thispassword`;
- the visible paired page objects are the yin-yang artifact;
- the creator-confirmed *Cosmic Duality* book is the reached artifact; or
- the `SalPhaseIon` title is the reached artifact.

The audit may classify a family as a backward echo, same-boundary
restatement, container/theme echo, or forward candidate. It may not invent a
new artifact or decoder.

## Outcomes

- **`consumer_promoted`** — exactly one family and one consumer clear all five
  gates.
- **`ambiguous_forward_roles`** — at least one family reaches the forward-role
  stage, but no unique executable consumer is fixed.
- **`recognition_only_no_forward_edge`** — convergence strengthens recognition
  semantics, but every family fails before an executable consumer edge.
- **`protocol_invalid`** — the Phase 423 commitments, candidate partition, or
  inherited audit facts drift.

The expected outcome is not frozen. The implementation must derive it from the
five gates and stop without an oracle.

## Interpretation limit

A negative result closes only the claim that Phase 423 convergence itself
selects a downstream consumer. It does not reject BYE/CIAO as recognition,
prove yin-yang unreached, solve FAED, or show that no future primary source can
fix the missing edge.
