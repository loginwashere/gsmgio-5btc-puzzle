# Phase 464 — Rotated-Prime Creator-Provenance Audit Protocol

## Question

Does any creator-authored evidence, fixed before inspection under this
protocol, independently select the load-bearing operations in the
`574061 -> 311027 -> THEFLOWER` recognition chain or identify an authenticated
consumer for its output?

This is a provenance audit, not a cipher, password, or arithmetic search.

## Frozen hypothesis

The candidate requires seven selectors:

1. rotate the 24 colored apertures by 90 degrees;
2. use complement/inverse polarity;
3. read both six-digit primes as forward 2x3 decimal matrices;
4. serialize each as `[total, row1, row2]` and index the Architect text;
5. use the total-selected word as an outer first/last-letter frame around the
   two row-word initials;
6. append the reversed even-position rail of `TRUE` to `FLOW`;
7. prefix the result with the selected word `THE`.

The known output `THEFLOWER`, its rose interpretation, and the failed bounded
continuation to `BLOSSOMS` are construction evidence, not held-out confirmation.

## Frozen selector vocabularies

Seven regex families correspond one-to-one with the required selectors:

- `S1_ROTATION`: rotate/rotation/turn/clockwise/counterclockwise/90 degrees/
  quarter-turn/grille/aperture;
- `S2_INVERSE`: inverse/invert/complement/polarity/opposite/dark-light/
  yellow-blue;
- `S3_PRIME_PAIR`: exact `311027` or `04BEF3`, second/two/both/pair of primes,
  or plural `primes`;
- `S4_MATRIX_LIST`: 2x3, matrix, row sum, total sum, `[total,row1,row2]`;
- `S5_FRAME`: outer frame, endpoints/edges, first and last, around, beginnings/
  endings;
- `S6_PARITY`: odd/even, parity, alternating/every second, reverse-even;
- `S7_FLOWER_PREFIX`: exact `THEFLOWER`, `THE FLOWER`, flower/rose, prefix,
  in front/before.

A separate `CONSUMER` family covers password/thispassword/key/enter/answer/
result/next phase/yin-yang/consumer. It cannot promote by itself.

## Retrieval and context rules

The executable audit must:

1. enumerate every creator-authored normal message in both corpora;
2. search creator text and the parent text of every direct creator reply;
3. for every licensed hit, preserve the creator message, parent, and all direct
   sibling replies to the same parent;
4. inventory every creator-authored media record, hash every available payload,
   retain captions/filenames, OCR still images, and sample video/animation
   frames at fixed fractions where supported;
5. report exact-number matches separately from vocabulary matches;
6. report non-creator corpus-wide match counts as descriptive base-rate
   controls, never as creator evidence;
7. make zero oracle calls and generate zero password candidates.

## Promotion gates

Promote the chain only if at least one condition holds:

1. `exact_reference`: creator-authored text/media independently contains
   `311027` or `04BEF3` in this puzzle context;
2. `two_selector_witness`: creator-authored text/media, or an unambiguously
   affirmative direct reply, selects `S1_ROTATION` plus at least one of
   `S2`–`S7` as operations on this first-piece chain;
3. `consumer_witness`: an authenticated artifact explicitly consumes the
   resulting `THEFLOWER`/`FLOWER`/`311027` output and validates it without a
   newly invented conversion.

Lexical co-occurrence, generic mentions, community-only proposals, emoji-only
reactions without a uniquely scoped parent, and known-password restatements do
not pass.

## Stop rule

If no promotion gate passes, retain `THEFLOWER` as a strong recognition
checkpoint and keep `G-FLOWER-001` parked. Do not extend to `BLOSSOMS`, open a
new password family, or pursue the 49-aperture grille.
