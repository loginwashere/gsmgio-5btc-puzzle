# Brainstorm — 2026-08-12 — Primary Evidence Acquisition

> [!caution] Incubation note
> This session is exploratory, not a finding or canonical evidence. Do not add
> it to `GSMG_HOME.md`'s canonical list. Promote surviving work through the
> governed path under [Promotion](#promotion).

## Desired outcome

Produce a ranked set of concrete, bounded ways to locate and authenticate new
primary artifacts capable of closing or materially advancing `G-MSL-001`,
`G-ESC-001`, or `G-YIN-001`. End with no more than three first experiments,
each with an explicit target, method, stopping rule, and success condition.

## Current understanding

### Known facts

- All three P0 gaps are locally exhausted; another recombination of the current
  corpus is not, by itself, new evidence.
- `G-MSL-001` needs a source binding the recovered 31-character selection to
  the missing `matrixsumlist` operation parameters. The physical *Cosmic
  Duality* pages 57–58 gatefold is the only known uninspected primary source.
- `G-ESC-001` needs an external source selecting or reconciling FAED's `{g,i}`
  pair and the Architect-mirror-predicted `{h,e}` pair. The known page markup
  and all five known Wayback captures are exhausted.
- `G-YIN-001` needs creator evidence or a structurally forced reading defining
  how DBBI and FAED interact. Existing Telegram corpora and decoded-slot page
  syntax are exhausted.

### Assumptions to challenge

- The missing evidence still exists somewhere.
- The book gatefold contains operational rather than merely thematic material.
- The five known Wayback captures cover every historically meaningful page
  state.
- Existing Telegram exports cover every creator-authored venue and reply.
- The three gaps require separate artifacts rather than one shared source.

### Constraints

- Acquisition must be lawful and respectful of privacy and access controls.
- Recollections and solver interpretations are leads, not primary evidence.
- A newly found artifact needs provenance and integrity checks before it can
  affect the governed record.
- Searches need budgets and stopping rules; absence from an archive is not
  proof that an artifact never existed.

### Unknowns

- Who currently owns a physical copy of *Cosmic Duality* with the gatefold?
- Whether alternate editions, previews, listings, or private photographs expose
  pages 57–58.
- Whether puzzle pages existed under alternate URLs, hosts, query strings, or
  mirrors not represented by the five captures.
- Whether creator messages survive in replies, forwards, exports, forums, or
  private collections outside the indexed corpora.
- Which early participants retained original files or contemporaneous notes.

## Divergence pass — raw idea inbox

Do not rank or reject during this pass.

### Physical-book lane

1. Identify every ISBN, edition, language, cover variant, publisher listing,
   and known seller record for *Cosmic Duality*.
2. Search used-book listings and archived listings for photographs that include
   the gatefold or page edges.
3. Locate libraries, special collections, review-copy recipients, and catalog
   records that may hold a physical copy.
4. Identify prior owners or readers from reviews, posts, photographs, and
   community discussions; ask only for pages 57–58 plus provenance context.
5. Create a minimal scanning brief specifying full spread, page edges, color
   reference, resolution, and an ownership/provenance photograph.

### Web-archive lane

6. Reconstruct the historical URL namespace from links, HTML, sitemaps, robots
   files, certificates, DNS history, and referring pages.
7. Enumerate semantically equivalent URL variants: scheme, `www`, host aliases,
   casing, trailing slash, filename, extension, query, and fragment-independent
   captures.
8. Check archives beyond the Wayback Machine and search-engine caches for page
   snapshots, source bundles, screenshots, or mirrors.
9. Search historical repositories, deployment artifacts, and downloaded site
   copies for SalPhaseIon HTML or related assets.
10. Identify early posts linking to the puzzle; their previews, embeds, or
    screenshots may preserve a different page state.

### Creator-communication lane

11. Build a venue inventory covering public channels, groups, replies, edits,
    forwards, forums, social accounts, interviews, and project announcements.
12. Compare export boundaries and message-ID gaps to identify periods or
    contexts absent from the current corpora.
13. Ask early participants for original exports or forwarded creator messages,
    keeping original metadata and surrounding context intact.
14. Search quoted fragments and distinctive puzzle terms for copies outside the
    original platform.
15. Draft non-leading outreach questions that request artifacts first and
    interpretations second.

### Cross-gap and provenance lane

16. Build a timeline aligning book publication, puzzle page changes, creator
    messages, and solver milestones to expose likely evidence-producing windows.
17. Build a participant/artifact map: who could plausibly possess which book,
    export, page capture, source file, screenshot, or download?
18. Search for one source package or contemporaneous solver archive that may
    contain both page files and creator communications.
19. Define an evidence intake packet: original file, acquisition path,
    timestamp, hash, metadata, contextual capture, and independent corroboration.
20. Pre-register what specific observation would advance each gap so an exciting
    artifact is not overinterpreted after discovery.

## Waiting-period backlog

These tracks are admissible because they seek new evidence or improve its
preservation. Re-running transformations over the current corpus is out of
scope unless a new artifact supplies a specific reopen condition.

### 1. Historical URL and archive surface map — highest discovery value

Build a finite inventory of every evidenced puzzle URL, hostname, path,
redirect, asset URL, and known capture date. Then query only genuinely new URL
variants and archives outside the already-diffed five captures.

**2026-08-12 verification:** substantially already completed by the existing
`gsmg-site-mirror` dump and `GSMG_PUZZLE.md` inventory. A fresh manifest,
source-map, and live-CDX comparison found bookkeeping defects but no uninspected
`matrixsumlist` implementation artifact. See
[[2026-08-12 - What Evidence Could Define Matrixsumlist#E1 local verification — 2026-08-12]].

- Deliverable: one search ledger with URL, archive/service, query date, result,
  and stopping rule.
- Could advance: `G-ESC-001`, possibly `G-YIN-001`.
- Guardrail: do not re-diff the five known SalPhaseIon captures.

### 2. Book-evidence intake tooling — highest readiness value

Prepare a small read-only intake command that inventories incoming image files,
records byte size and SHA-256, extracts available metadata, and emits a manifest
without altering originals.

- Deliverable: reproducible manifest plus clearly separated derivative area.
- Could advance: authentication of `G-MSL-001` evidence.
- Guardrail: no OCR, recompression, rotation, or metadata edits to originals.

### 3. Creator-venue coverage map — medium discovery value

List every evidenced creator venue and the exact date/message-ID coverage of
each local export. Mark missing periods, reply contexts, edit history, and
platforms rather than searching the same decoded text again.

- Deliverable: coverage table and a shortlist of genuinely absent sources.
- Could advance: `G-YIN-001` and `G-ESC-001`.
- Guardrail: distinguish a missing source from a keyword miss inside an already
  exhausted source.

**2026-08-12 E2 verification:** both current Telegram exports contain every
file named by every surviving media or thumbnail field, including all
creator-authored and directly creator-connected media. The residual is 39
deleted reply parents, not a missing-file manifest; most are contextually
non-puzzle, and the sole already-known ambiguous rabbit-thread record is
creator reply `28548` to deleted parent `28547`. See
[[2026-08-12 - What Evidence Could Define Matrixsumlist#E2 local verification — 2026-08-12]].

### 4. Non-leading outreach packet — contingent value

Draft a short request for original files, exports, screenshots, or book-page
images, together with provenance questions. Do not name desired interpretations
before the artifact is supplied.

- Deliverable: reusable request plus artifact-authentication checklist.
- Could advance: all three P0 gaps if a contact later appears.
- Guardrail: recollection alone remains a lead, not primary evidence.

### 5. Arrival-day dry run — low discovery, low effort

Test the gatefold capture protocol on another folded book or document. Check
focus, glare, edge visibility, overlap, scale, color reference, file transfer,
and hashing before the real artifact arrives.

- Deliverable: corrected capture procedure and known-good setup.
- Could advance: capture quality for `G-MSL-001`.
- Guardrail: use the rehearsal only to validate technique, not to invent
  interpretive criteria.
