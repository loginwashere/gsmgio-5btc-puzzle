(`doc/GSMG_BRAINSTORM_BACKLOG_LEDGER.md`, "Recommended next bounded

**Disposition:** negative for this exact 83-candidate manifest (7 site
assets + 76 unique creator-media payloads), three byte forms,

document's own bounding rules, on a materially larger, more precisely
described, and content-pinned universe versus the first draft. Does not
extend to the excluded files above (no evidence bars them, only the
