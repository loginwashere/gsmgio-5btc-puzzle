## Phase 550 — board-profiled frequency test of FAED, compatible with low power (2026-09-29)

**Question:** Can FAED's sorted symbol counts (invariant to any transposition) be produced by a
nine-symbol straddling checkerboard over letters-only English for any board the search reaches?

**Frozen inputs:** 7+9+9 board, `J -> I`; corpora K1 *Cosmic Duality* book, K2 deduplicated Matrix
screenplays, K3 the creator's solved English. `T` = board-profiled sorted Pearson X2. Annealing plus
2-swap polish (not exhaustive). 200 simulations at a VIC board, five random boards and the fitted
board; `p` = maximum over them; Holm over the corpora, bar 0.01.

**Method:** controls before the lock. The search was not worse than the true board in 100/100
planted cases per corpus; size was 0-2.5%. Power at 0.01/3 was 0.85-1.00 against uniform, 0-0.18
against a merged-decimal profile and 0.02-0.25 against hex boards.

**Result:** compatible for K1 (p 0.567), K2 (0.940) and K3 (0.592); Holm 1.0. Best-board fits are
near-exact (T about 0.01, typical of the simulations). DBBI (reported only): 0.97 / 0.97 / 0.37.

**Disposition:** bounded and uninformative against skewed alternatives. Counts alone cannot
exclude a transposed checkerboard over English or distinguish it from other skewed sources. Phase
549's transposition gap stays open; nothing is promoted.

**Facts affected:** None.

**Supersedes/corrects:** None. Follow-up to Phase 549.

**Reopen condition:** a restricted, independently selected transposition class or a new
transposition-invariant non-count statistic; not added corpora or boards on this run.
