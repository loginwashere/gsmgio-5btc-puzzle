## Phase 224 -- `yinyang` does not redirect SalPhaseIon instructions to COSMIC (2026-08-10)

Tested the narrow interpretation that the creator's “It's the next phase”
labels the next visible page section, making a password recovered from
SalPhaseIon apply to the following Cosmic Duality ciphertext. The audit gives
the two falsifiable claims promotion gates and keeps semantic resonance and DOM
adjacency at their proper lower weights.

The first gate fails structurally. Inside the single authenticated SalPhaseIon
textarea, `thispassword` and `sha256` immediately precede the SALPH blob, while
the binary `enter` instruction splits its two 64-character halves. No markup or
decoded instruction redirects that local sequence to the separate COSMIC
textarea. A cross-textarea consumer therefore does not displace the nearer
SALPH-self reading.

The complete creator export also supplies no usage precedent for interpreting
“next phase” as “next DOM section.” There are 13 creator messages containing a
singular `phase`, `stage`, `page`, or `section`; all five exact `next
phase`/`next stage` messages (3348, 4694, 4771, 39237, 60314) describe solver
progression, with zero `next page` or `next section` uses. Message 39237 is a
direct reply to “is yinyang found after decoding an AES ciphertext?”, but its
wording neither answers the AES premise yes/no nor points to a page element.

The semantic evidence is nevertheless real. Pages 8–9 of the authenticated
book transcript explicitly say yin and yang embody duality and that equal yin
and yang form cosmic balance. COSMIC is also an authenticated page blob. The
contamination guard is therefore deliberately narrow: it excludes only Phase
210's exact seven-token XOR → raw32/MD5 → 103×103 → base-38 pipeline, checkpoint
hash, and two derived addresses, not COSMIC itself.

**Verdict:** do not promote SalPhaseIon → COSMIC. The semantic book/title match
is supportive and DOM adjacency is necessary-but-trivial, but both promotion
gates fail: the nearer SALPH-self binding remains unexplained and creator usage
does not equate solve progression with document order.
