## Phase 260 -- Cosmic Duality title page's C/D initials: pixel-confirmed miniature yin-yang glyphs, Roman CD=400 independently echoes the yellow prime sum (2026-08-13)

User-flagged observation on the book's interior title page (one of the same
73 2026-07-12 source photos behind `cosmic_duality_book_full_text.txt`,
`~/Pictures/Screenshots/Screenshot from 2026-07-12 14-41-28.png`, sha256
`4493e8bd47a56d004bba4a4dd33bf077212892e89150807e02800bda8864e86b`): the
initial letters `C` (Cosmic) and `D` (Duality) are rendered as miniature
yin-yang glyphs -- a gold/black curved split with inverse dots -- while
every other letter in the title is plain black.

**Pixel-verified, not eyeballed.** `tools/gsmg/cosmic_duality_title_initials_yinyang_audit.py`
scans the title region for gold-ink pixels (independently re-derived with
two differently-thresholded gold-detection definitions, both agreeing):
gold appears only in two narrow, isolated column ranges -- x=955-983
(inside "Cosmic," exactly the `C` glyph's box) and x=1146-1181 (inside
"Duality," exactly the `D` glyph's box) -- with zero gold pixels anywhere
else across the full "Cosmic Duality" title, including all of "osmic" and
"uality." The same title also appears on the book's front cover (a
different photo, `Screenshot from 2026-07-12 14-41-07.png`, sha256
`fadab2e6b753030e9e807e70fa52a2d7503b573ac736a6f14fa1f3e1579a6c8c`), in the
same decorative serif typeface, but rendered
entirely in plain uniform silver foil -- zero gold pixels anywhere in that
rendering. This rules out "quirk of this font" and confirms the yin-yang
treatment is a deliberate choice specific to the interior title-page
typesetting.

**Numerology, exact and standard, not fitted.** Reading `CD` as a Roman
numeral gives the ordinary subtractive value `500 - 100 = 400` -- no
alternate parsing chosen after the fact. This exactly matches the
already-independently-derived yellow color-event prime sum `400` from
`first_piece_prime_sum_reconstruction.py` (Phase 196's `401/400/73`
blue/yellow/FEFE triple). Controls checked and none land on an
established value: `DC` (additive order) = `600`; hex `CD` = `205`; ASCII
sum `C+D` = `135`; A1Z26 sum `C+D` = `7`.

**What this does and does not establish.** It upgrades `400` from an
internally-fitted structural value (Phase 196's spiral-prime-assignment
result) to one independently echoed by an authenticated physical artifact
-- a real, meaningful strengthening of confidence that `400` specifically
(not an adjacent prime) is significant. It does **not** supply a G3
operation: no creator source instructs "read the title's decorated
initials as a Roman numeral," and this does not touch the blue (`401`) or
FEFE (`73`) members of the triple, nor open a consumer for any of them.
It reinforces, rather than newly establishes, `yinyang_artifact_inventory_audit.py`'s
existing `cosmic_duality_book` qualification entry ("creator-confirmed
physical clue with explicit yin/yang content, but no established
operation"). `spi_cd_initials_audit.py` had already tested `CD` as a
password-abbreviation component (`SPICD`, elemental `Cd`, etc.) but never
as a Roman numeral -- a genuine gap in that earlier audit, now closed by
this observation rather than by that script.

No broad oracle sweep was run: `400`, `CD`, and `"Cosmic Duality"` are not
