## Phase 192 -- URLBLOB promoted into the default target registry; post-quarantine coverage inventory and bounded rerun (2026-08-08)

URLBLOB's provenance remains weaker than the other three targets, but target
inclusion and provenance confidence are separate questions. Its bytes were
independently recovered from a real archived `gsmg.io` URL in Phase 27, and
there are only four known open blobs. Excluding it from `cb_common.BLOBS`
therefore created an easy-to-miss coverage gap in every later audit that used
the shared default without explicitly merging `QUARANTINED_BLOBS`.

The pre-change static inventory found 54 Python modules referencing `BLOBS`:

- 34 also mentioned `QUARANTINED_BLOBS`, although often only behind an opt-in
  flag;
- 20 did not mention the quarantine registry at all;
- of those 20, 16 are general candidate/oracle consumers and therefore had a
  real URLBLOB coverage gap;
- four are deliberately target- or shape-specific
  (`binary_key_material_backfill.py`, `lastcommand_probe.py`,
  `nopad_window_sweep.py`, and `staged_pipeline.py`) and were not mechanically
  broadened merely to make a count larger.

`cb_common.BLOBS` now contains, in stable order, `SALPH`, `COSMIC`,
`P32TRAILING`, and `URLBLOB`. `QUARANTINED_BLOBS["URLBLOB"]` remains as a
compatibility and provenance-specific view of the exact same tuple; older
`{**BLOBS, **QUARANTINED_BLOBS}` code remains harmless. This makes URLBLOB
automatic in all future general-purpose password, KDF, raw-key, and scalar
candidate checks without falsely claiming equal puzzle-role corroboration.

The promotion exposed and corrected three stale cardinality assumptions:

1. `cosmic_83_guide_alignment_audit.py` now keeps the historical `6/84/6`
   structural premise explicitly scoped to SALPH/COSMIC/P32TRAILING while its
   cryptographic consumers use all four defaults. URLBLOB's seven-block
   salted envelope is not silently forced into a geometry it did not define.
2. `salt_phase_ion_audit.py` now includes URLBLOB's salt, literal-XOR body,
   candidate evaluation, and six-block ciphertext count. Its 80-byte-only
   matrix gate correctly remains SALPH/P32TRAILING.
3. `salt_selector_permutation_audit.py` expands from four salts including the
   solved control to five, and from 192 to 240 structural outputs. The live
   Bonferroni label is now derived from that count instead of hard-coded.

Rerun results: all 22 recent regression tests pass, and 43 applicable bounded
self-test/audit paths across the shared-registry consumers were exercised
without an unexpected real-target hit. The newly expanded salt selector ran
`5 salts x 4 sources x 12 rules = 240` outputs and 300 exact bound
passphrases; its best URLBLOB row had uncorrected `p=0.034483`, non-significant
after the declared family correction, and the exact oracle had zero hits.

Two small/current-corpus gaps were also rerun in full after promotion:

- 13 chat-mined command-provenance candidates x 24 cipher/KDF variants x 4
  blobs: zero hits;
- the current 648-candidate whitespace/newline corpus (digest
  `2d233645ef49a141`) produced 22,716 attempts across 18 extended cipher/KDF
  variants x 4 blobs: zero hits.

The second run matters because Phase 27's URLBLOB sweep used the older
568-candidate corpus; it now covers the expanded candidate set and its exact
whitespace variants under the current default registry.

This closes the *bounded recent-audit* omission. It does not claim that every
historical long-running corpus has been recomputed after promotion: the large
chain-addition/checkpoint sweep and expensive full-corpus cipher/Key-Wrap
jobs should be treated as explicit URL-only backfills, not silently restarted
as part of this registry change.
