## Phase 448 -- brute-force eligibility audit: no clue-supported finite search space (2026-08-29)

**Question:** does any remaining clue-supported construction define a finite
search space that is eligible for brute force?

**Method:** froze seven conjunctive gates before classification: clue-selected
operands, fixed operation/domain, serialization, consumer, validator,
unresolved status, and cardinality calculable without analyst-added bounds.
Audited every one of the nine open-gap registry entries plus Phase 446's P32
residual classes, panel resampling, and the Architect brute-force sentence.
Phase 441's completed exact `16!` run was retained as a closed positive
control. No candidate generation or oracle work was permitted.

**Result:** 0 of 15 rows passes all seven gates. Six have finite local
skeletons (`G-ESC-001`, `G-PRIME-001`, `G-MATPROD-001`, `G-KIT-001`,
`G-X2SH-001`, and P32 F09), but the first five lack clue-fixed downstream
operations/consumers/validators. F09 passes 6/7 and is approximately 700,000
keystrings, yet remains curated coverage debt rather than a clue-selected
construction. The Architect sentence supplies only a possible method: it
specifies no operand, domain, serialization, consumer, validator, or count.
Phase 441's 20,922,789,888,000-case domain is complete and negative, so it is
not a remaining space.

**Disposition:** `no_clue_supported_finite_search_space`. Do not launch a new
brute-force run from current evidence. Reopen only when independent evidence
lets one construction pass all seven gates; countability alone is
insufficient. Zero password materials, zero oracle calls, and Docker/GPU/
network/external agents untouched.
