`tools/gsmg/first_piece_png_palette_provenance_audit.py` parses the raw full

IHDR fields, IDAT decompression, scanline filters, terminal IEND, and decoded

```text
IHDR, sRGB, gAMA, pHYs, IDAT, IEND
```
