## Phase 547 — nine-symbol origin census over DBBI and FAED, closed null-like (2026-09-28)

**Question:** Do any native nine-symbol producers that were never tested (keyed Trifid coordinates,
phone multi-tap, Morbit, Pollux) explain DBBI's and FAED's `a..i` alphabet?

**Frozen inputs:** M1 = all 9! symbol-to-trit-pair maps x offsets 0/1/2, key-invariant 27-cell IoC (FAED
gated, DBBI reported); M2 = 9 x 8! multi-tap maps, conventions C1 (pause) and C2 (space), normalized
quadgram score (FAED and DBBI gated); M3/M4 = Morbit 9! and Pollux 3^9 maps, exact Morse validity, forward
and reversed. Shuffle nulls 1,000 (M1) and 500 (M2); Holm over three cells, bar 0.01. Lock pins protocol,
script, test, data, quadgrams, planted-text source, seeds and controls.

**Method:** Controls before the lock: vectorized vs direct M1/M2, planted Trifid (5 texts), planted
multi-tap (C1, C2) and planted Morbit/Pollux all recovered with wide margins.

**Result:** M1-FAED IoC 0.0444 (null median 0.0433), p 0.098, Holm 0.196; M2-FAED p 0.856; M2-DBBI p 0.012,
Holm 0.036 (decode is not text); M1-DBBI p 0.447 (not gated). No C1-valid map for either stream. Morbit and
Pollux: 0 valid maps in all four streams. All gated cells `null_like`; census verdict
`no_untested_native_nine_producer_in_census`.

**Disposition:** closed negative within the declared census; these four members are not the origin of the
nine-symbol alphabet.

**Facts affected:** None.

**Supersedes/corrects:** None. Extends Phase 277 (offset 0, fixed alphabets) to all maps, offsets and cubes.
