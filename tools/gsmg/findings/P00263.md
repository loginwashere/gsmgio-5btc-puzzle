## Phase 263 -- first-piece Roman-rail prime-sum audit: `C+DI=CDI=401` and `C+D=CD=400` exactly reproduce the ordered color sums, but a second control hit and unresolved FEFE/73 prevent promotion (2026-08-13)

The user asked where else Roman numerals could be interpreted after the
Phase 260/261 `CD=400` observation. The strongest bounded construction uses
the literal beginnings of the two authenticated raw streams:

```text
DBBI -> keep Roman letters -> DI
FAED -> keep Roman letters -> D

C + DI -> CDI = 401
C + D  -> CD  = 400
```

Thus the assignment `blue -> DBBI`, `yellow -> FAED` reproduces the fitted
prime sums in their exact order: blue `401`, yellow `400`. Both numerals are
strict canonical Roman forms; permissive forms such as `IC=99` are rejected.
This is stronger than merely noticing that the title initials `CD` equal 400:
the extra `I` genuinely distinguishes the two rails and the observed color
ordering selects DBBI for the 401 rail and FAED for the 400 rail.

`roman_rail_prime_sum_audit.py` fixes a nonredundant family before evaluating
it: both DBBI/FAED color polarities, and the empty string or every nonempty
order-preserving subset of title initials `CD` placed before or after the
Roman projection. Exactly **1 of 14** rail-family configurations hits ordered
`(401,400)`: DBBI/FAED with `C` prefixed, producing `CDI/CD`.

The broader disclosed sensitivity control is important. Across 13
authenticated/high-salience puzzle labels and macro tokens, all 156 ordered
token pairs, and the same seven title contexts (**1,092 configurations**),
there are **two**, not one, ordered hits:

```text
DBBI / FAED    with prefix C  -> CDI / CD -> 401 / 400
yinyang / FEFE with prefix CD -> CDI / CD -> 401 / 400
```

The second row results because `roman(yinyang)=I` and `roman(FEFE)` is empty.
It is not a solution: it arbitrarily assigns `yinyang` and FEFE to blue/yellow
and, especially, fails to consume FEFE's actual fitted sum `73`. But it proves
that the numeral pair is not globally unique even in a small relevant-token
control set. The 1/14 and 2/1,092 figures are bounded-family accounting, not
chance probabilities; the token set is salient and disclosed, not randomly
sampled.

Two selectors remain absent: no creator instruction says to keep only Roman
letters, and none says to use only the title's `C` rather than `D` or `CD`.
The construction also leaves the third member of `401/400/73` unexplained.
**Verdict:** a real exact structural correspondence and the best Roman lead
found so far, but corroboration only. It does not supply G-PRIME-001's missing
consumer or G-YIN-001's DBBI/FAED operator, and no password sweep is warranted.
