## Phase 181 -- movie-transcript-vs-shooting-script check on the `[23,16,7]` source text (2026-08-08)

This project's only local Architect-scene source
(`wordlists/gsmg/matrix_script_windows.txt`, and the frozen
`matrix_architect_scene_through_choice_words.txt` derived from it) is drawn
from the numbered shooting script -- its own first line self-identifies as
`"...NUMBERED SHOOTING SCRIPT March..."` -- not the released film. Question:
could the theatrical dialogue differ from the shooting-script draft at a
point that matters to the established `[23,16,7] -> BOTH/ULTIMATELY/THE ->
BUT/HYE` derivation (Phase 33) or the word-position work built on the
1,326-word scene cache (Phase 139's `401/400/73` indices, Phase 174's
salt-selector audit)?

No local subtitle or movie transcript file exists anywhere in this repo
(checked directly -- every Matrix-related source here is script-derived).
Checked two independently-hosted movie transcripts against the local script
text instead, using narrow "list only the differences" queries to stay
within fair use rather than reproducing full passages:

- [The Architect Transcript](https://scottmanning.com/content/the-architect-transcript/) (scottmanning.com)
- [The Matrix Reloaded script](https://www.springfieldspringfield.co.uk/movie_script.php?movie=matrix-reloaded-the) (springfieldspringfield.co.uk, subtitle-derived)

Cross-referenced against two quote-aggregator pages
([quotes.net/mquote/121335](https://www.quotes.net/mquote/121335),
[quotes.net/mquote/60110](https://www.quotes.net/mquote/60110)) for a third
opinion where a discrepancy surfaced.

**1. The exact block the `[23,16,7]` indexing actually uses is unaffected.**
*"Which brings us at last to the moment of truth, wherein the fundamental
flaw is ultimately expressed, and the anomaly revealed as both beginning and
end."* matches word-for-word, including punctuation, against scottmanning.
com's transcript -- zero differences. `BOTH`/`ULTIMATELY`/`THE` ->
`BUT`/`HYE` (Phase 33) rests on text confirmed identical between the
shooting-script draft and the released film.

**2. A real draft-to-final change exists later in the same speech, confirmed
by two independent sources.** Shooting script: *"The door to your right will
take you to the Source and the salvation of Zion."* Film (scottmanning.com
and springfieldspringfield.co.uk agree): *"The door to your right leads to
the source, and the salvation of Zion."* A four-word phrase shortened to
two.

**3. Checked impact on existing results -- none.** The discrepancy sits at
word index 1282 (0-based) in the cached 1,326-word file. Phase 139's indices
(`401`, `400`, `73`) all resolve well before this point. Phase 174's
salt-byte selectors are always `byte % 1326` for a single salt byte
(0-255), so they never reach index 1282 regardless. No existing finding
requires correction. Any future index-based work over this cached file that
reaches past word ~1280 should know the text is a shooting-script reading at
that one spot, not the released film's.

**4. One apparent second discrepancy did not hold up.** quotes.net's two
pages both read *"blinding you to the simple and obvious truth,"* differing
from the local script's *"blinding you from..."*. Checked against the two
independent transcript sources instead of the quote-aggregator: both
scottmanning.com and springfieldspringfield.co.uk confirm *"from,"* matching
the local script. quotes.net is the outlier here, consistent with this
project's standing skepticism toward quote-aggregator/community-paraphrase
sources over primary transcripts (same discipline as Phase 56's retracted
mis-attribution and Phase 174's source-description correction).

**Verdict:** the shooting-script source this project's established
`[23,16,7]` derivation depends on is confirmed identical to the released
film at the specific sentence it indexes. One genuine shooting-script/film
wording difference exists later in the same scene, but it falls outside
every word-position range this project's existing phases actually use, so
nothing already logged needs revision. This was a provenance/robustness
check, not a new candidate family -- not escalated to AES.
