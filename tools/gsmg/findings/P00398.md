## Phase 398 -- BTCSEED/P91/Z brainstorm Priority 2: BIP39 checksum recalibration under the two grid-native mappings, closed negative (2026-08-25)

**Question:** Priority 2 of the 2026-08-25 BTCSEED/P91/Z continuation
brainstorm asks for an evidential recalibration, not a new wallet sweep:
Phase 394 tested all 24 permutations of `{b,c,d,e}->{0,1,2,3}` (3,696
trials, 13 checksum-valid). Only 2 of those 24 are "square-native" reads
of the keyed square's upper-left `2x2` (`D,B,C,E` / `D,C,B,E`). Under just
those two, is the previously-posted mnemonic still one of roughly the
expected count of checksum-valid hits, does Bifid's own coordinate
convention favor one mapping over the other, and does anything
independently select the posted offset 30? Address/path authentication is
explicitly excluded -- Phase 394 already tested the posted mnemonic
against 12,000 derivation checks and found nothing, and that result is not
re-litigated here.

**Method:** wrote
`tools/gsmg/phase398_p91z_priority2_bip39_recalibration_audit.py`, reusing
Phase 394's own `load_bip39_words()`, `bits_to_mnemonic()`,
`bip39_checksum_valid()`, and rail extraction verbatim. Row-major
(`D,B,C,E->0,1,2,3`) and column-major (`D,C,B,E->0,1,2,3`, algebraically
confirmed identical to Phase 394's posted `TARGET_MAPPING`) x 154 windows
(`285-132+1`) = 308 trials, enumerated before any output was inspected.
Two closed secondary checks: (1) which mapping requires no axis swap of
the keyed square's own `(row, col)` coordinate order (the same order this
project's `bifid_decrypt()` already uses everywhere) -- row-major reads
`value = row*2+col` directly; column-major requires swapping which axis is
read first; (2) whether offset 30 matches any of 6 pre-declared,
boundary-derived rail offsets (rail start/end, `Q472`/`P91` boundary
positions, window-count/rail-length midpoints) -- fixed before checking,
not tuned to hit 30 afterward.

**Result:** 308 trials, expected-under-uniform `308/256 ~= 1.203`. Exactly
**1** checksum-valid mnemonic reproduces -- the same previously-posted one,
under column-major (the posted mapping) at offset 30; row-major produces
**zero** hits across its 154 windows. Offset 30 matches **none** of the 6
pre-declared natural-boundary candidates.

**Disposition:** closes per the brainstorm's own stated criterion -- the
mnemonic is one of the expected ~1.2 checksum-valid results under the
reduced family (not a surprising excess), and offset 30 remains
unselected by any pre-declared boundary. Row-major (the axis-swap-free,
more mechanically native reading) produces no candidate at all, so there
is no competing hit to weigh it against. Column-major's checkpoint status
is unchanged from Phase 394: mechanically real, still not independently
selected, still not authenticated. Phase 394's address/path negative
stands untouched.

**Artifacts:**
`tools/gsmg/phase398_p91z_priority2_bip39_recalibration_audit.py`;
permanent regression in `tools/gsmg/test_recent_audits.py`.
