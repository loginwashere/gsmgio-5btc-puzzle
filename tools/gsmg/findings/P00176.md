## Phase 176 -- every-`i` immediate-neighbor channels over DBBI/FAED (2026-08-08)

Phase 170 formally counted the title-level I/eye positions and recorded their
immediate flanks as `EO | MC | LT`, but the broader proposal to extract the
neighbors of every `i` in the two unresolved `a-i` streams had never been run.
Implemented `tools/gsmg/eye_neighbor_channel_audit.py` with no chosen positions:
every internal lowercase `i` contributes its immediately preceding and following
character in source order.

The audit freezes the six canonical representations of each paired stream:

```text
left rail
right rail
alternating left-right
alternating right-left
left || right
right || left
```

DBBI contains five `i`s, all internal; FAED contains 75, all internal. DBBI's
complete outputs are small enough to show directly:

```text

right:           bhhbg
alternating LR:  bbbhehcbgg
alternating RL:  bbhbhebcgg
```

A deterministic 2,000-trial null shuffled only the non-`i` symbols while
holding every `i` position fixed. English quadgram scoring found a minimum
uncorrected upper-tail probability of `0.109445` (FAED alternating LR), which
corrects to `1.0` across the twelve layouts. Two structural paired-channel
checks were equally ordinary:

```text
             equal pairs (p)       mirror9 pairs (p)
DBBI          2 (0.195902)           1 (0.503248)
FAED         11 (0.235882)           4 (0.964018)
```

The title control reconstructs the earlier pairs exactly. Read as two combined
rails across `SalPhaseIon` then `Cosmic Duality`, they are `EML | OCT`. Because
`OCT` could suggest octal, the audit includes one literal feasibility check
rather than leaving another untested word association: remove `i`, map `a-h`
to digits `0-7`, and require fixed three-octal-digit bytes, or treat each `i` as
a separator. The first rule leaves DBBI at 86 symbols (`86 mod 3 = 2`) while
FAED has 495 (`mod 3 = 0`); only 12/28 and 59/165 complete triples respectively
fall in byte range. The separator segments are irregular for both sources
(FAED includes 13 empty segments). Thus `OCT` does not supply a shared,
zero-parameter octal byte framing. No offsets, padding, or digit remaps followed.

Each of the twelve exact channel strings was tried verbatim as a passphrase.
Their SHA-256 values, plus first/last 32 bytes where available, produced 24
forms / 20 unique 32-byte materials and were tested as passphrases, raw keys,
and secp256k1 scalars. Phase 3.2 remains an AES-oracle positive control, not a
known-answer calibration for an I-neighbor mechanism that has no authenticated
Phase 3.2 analogue.


**Verdict:** close the literal every-`i` immediate-neighbor channel family.
Neither channel has language or yin-yang mirror structure beyond fixed-position
shuffle noise, none reaches an oracle, and the title's incidental `OCT` rail
does not frame both source streams as octal. A wider neighbor radius, selective
`i` subset, filtering rule, checkerboard keyword, or cross-source splice would
add an unfixed parameter and needs new primary evidence.
