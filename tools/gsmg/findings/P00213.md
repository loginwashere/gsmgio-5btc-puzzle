## Phase 213 -- page-syntax scope audit: exact `matrixsumlist`/`enter` island parallel does not fix operand scope (2026-08-09)

With the physical book still in transit and no community query planned,
`tools/gsmg/matrixsumlist_page_scope_audit.py` tested the only remaining
source-native G3 reduction without executing a transform. The authenticated
SalPhaseIon stream contains exactly two pure `a/b` binary-ASCII instruction
islands:

```text
DBBI(91) [matrixsumlist: 104 bits] FAED(570)
AES(64)  [enter: 40 bits]          AES(64)
```

The visual/encoding parallel is exact. Its semantics are not. Removing
`enter` joins equal 64-character Base64 pieces into an independently valid
96-byte OpenSSL object. DBBI and FAED are unequal, separately named a-i
streams and have no independently validated join. The page also demonstrably
uses mixed instruction positions: `sha256 our first hint...` precedes a
payload; `lastwordsbeforearchichoice`, `thispassword`, and unresolved `anstoo`
follow payload material; the two binary instructions lie between regions.
Position alone therefore does not define fixity.

All three Phase-101 roles remain syntactically possible: postfix to DBBI,
prefix to FAED, and infix between DBBI/FAED. The recovered guide gives the
postfix reading historical community support, but cannot select creator intent:
its segmentation was fitted to prime positions, its author reports no FAED
pattern and no further result, and its output opens no authenticated stage.

**Verdict:** the binary-island parallel is retained as a real authored
structural feature, but it does not reduce G3 to one operation. No matrix
transform or oracle is admitted. The transition remains evidence-blocked.
