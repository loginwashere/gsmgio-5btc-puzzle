## Phase 234 -- CIAO selection-and-coverage audit: no creator selection, direct-password gap closed (2026-08-10)

`tools/gsmg/ciao_selection_coverage_audit.py` corrects an over-broad
`BELLA -> {b,e}` reading proposed after Phase 233 and replaces it with two
narrow checks: a two-corpus creator search and a precise coverage census.

The `{b,e}` reading is withdrawn: filtering `BELLA` to the native `a-i`
alphabet gives three letters (`bea`), not a pair. Selecting `{b,e}` because it
matches DBBI's already-known best pair is a post-hoc choice among three, not
a mechanical two-letter output or a third independent convergence.

The two-corpus creator search widens Phase 233's `ciao`-only, solver-export-only
search to both corpora and to `ciao`, `bella`, `bye`, `goodbye`, `yin`, `yang`,
`yinyang`, and `password`. The support-group export adds zero new `ciao`,
`bella`, or `yin`/`yang` mentions from the creator. Four incidental hits
(`4272` a bot command, `66909` a BIP 360 explainer, `58072` a scammer
dismissal, `52876` an account-support question) are all classified irrelevant
on inspection. The creator-selection gate stays closed on both corpora.

The coverage census finds `ciaobella`/`ciaobellao` already tested as
checkerboard keywords twice (Phase 2's `cosmic_sweep.py` dictionary sweep,
SALPH+COSMIC only; and `alphabet_hypothesis_check.py`'s direct `pad28`
check) -- both negative. `ciao`, `bella`, `obellaciao`, and `bellaciao` had no
prior coverage of any kind. None of the five had ever been tested as a
literal direct blob password.

A bounded direct-password check (the same 18-keystring-form pattern Phase 232
used for `bye`) closes that specific gap for all five candidates against all
four tracked blobs: 90 keystrings x 4 blobs, 0 hits.

Explicitly not run: song-lyric indexing under `[23,16,7]` (no creator
instruction binds those indices to *Bella Ciao*, unlike the macro's literal
`lastwordsbeforearchichoice` binding to the Architect text, and the song has
multiple verse versions); autokey/chain-addition seeding (needs a frozen
algorithm/normalization first); and checkerboard-keyword coverage of
`ciaobella`/`ciaobellao` against P32TRAILING/URLBLOB (Phase 2 predates those
blobs -- flagged as a residual gap, not closed).
