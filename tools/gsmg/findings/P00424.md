## Phase 424 -- Phase-423 semantic checkpoint consumer audit: closed `recognition_only_no_forward_edge` (2026-08-27)

**Question:** Phase 423 produced exact convergence on `BYE`, `CIAOBELLAO`,
`SALVATION`, and the Architect rails, but every comparison-new candidate was
oracle-negative. Does that agreement instead identify a unique, authenticated
recognition artifact or executable downstream consumer?

**Method:** froze all 12 Phase-423 promotions into five exhaustive semantic
families: farewell, title-salvation, Architect rails, creator macro literals,
and solved-tail extraction. Each family had to pass five gates: source
grounding, correct boundary, evidence independent of the shared panel prompt,
a unique referent, and a deterministic executable consumer. The consumer
universe was limited to six roles already present in the page, yin-yang
inventory, SALVATION-role, and post-yinyang dataflow audits. The implementation
pins the exact Phase-423 result and the older artifact-inventory source, uses
its frozen qualification table because its retired `_work` transcript path is
no longer present, and invokes no cipher or password oracle.

**Result:** the farewell family is the strongest recognition echo. `BYE` and
`CIAOBELLAO` each received 5/5 Phase-423 votes, and historical community
messages independently linked HYE/BYE, the authenticated `CIAO BELLA O` tail,
and goodbye/hello duality before the modern HYE-to-BYE audit. It passes source,
boundary, and independent-discriminator gates. It fails unique-referent and
executable-consumer gates: no creator message selects the translation, no
deterministic `BYE -> CIAO` operation exists, and no downstream consumer is
fixed. The other four families stop earlier as a container/theme echo,
same-boundary restatement, prompt-literal cluster, or backward solved-output
echo. Zero of five families and zero of six consumer roles were promoted.
There were zero oracle calls.

**Branch:** `recognition_only_no_forward_edge`.

**Interpretation:** Phase 423 adds reproducibility to the farewell semantic
neighborhood, not a causal puzzle edge. The most local live role remains
`FAED -> decoded plaintext -> thispassword`, with the FAED decoder unknown;
the less-local DBBI/FAED joint role remains operator-unknown. Further variants
of BYE/CIAO/SALVATION/rails/macro literals are not licensed. New evidence must
constrain a FAED decoder or explicitly define the DBBI/FAED relationship.
