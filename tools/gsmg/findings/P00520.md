## Phase 520 -- DBBI+FAED joint-splice hypothesis, closed negative (2026-09-17)

**Question:** DBBI and FAED share one text node with no markup boundary,
joined only by the `matrixsumlist` instruction span (Phase 101/243-244) --
structurally like a PEM/base64 blob split by an inline marker. Treating
`matrixsumlist` as a pure splice marker rather than a real instruction, does
decoding `DBBI + FAED` as one continuous 661-symbol raw checkerboard stream
reveal coherence that decoding the two streams separately does not? Distinct
from Phase 412 (shared-vs-independent statistical parameters), Phase 371
(instruction-embeds-content check), and Phase 518 (proportional positional
correspondence) -- none of which tested a literal joint-decode reading.

**Method:** `tools/gsmg/phase520_dbbi_faed_joint_splice_hypothesis_audit.py`.
Three checks: (1) is `661 = len(DBBI)+len(FAED)` prime, excluding a
single-transposition-grid reading under this project's own established
width-admissibility convention; (2) the spliced stream's own single best
code-IC fit across all 36 escape pairs, calibrated against 20,000-replicate
matched nulls reusing Phase 459/460's exact Euler-traversal and
endpoint-fixed-shuffle surrogate generators; (3) substitution-only
board-anneal decode of the splice at its own best pair (no transposition,
per check 1), using Phase 516's validated solver, compared against each
stream's own established best-pair decode at the same budget.

**Result:** (1) 661 is prime, zero nontrivial factor pairs -- no
rectangular transposition grid applies to the full splice. (2) The
spliced stream's best pair (`eg`, page order) fits *worse* than either
individual stream's own optimum (`be` for DBBI, `gi` for FAED); real loss
`0.010081` sits at the primary null's median (`p=0.429829`) and is worse
than 97% of the sensitivity null (`p=0.973651`). Decision:
`no_calibrated_joint_coherence`. (3) Board-anneal: the `DBBI+FAED` splice's
normalized quadgram score (`-5.714`) is the worst of four candidates
(DBBI alone `-4.754`, FAED alone `-5.572`, reverse splice `-5.570`); no
preview is English or word-like.

**Disposition:** closed negative on all three checks, mutually reinforcing.
Corroboration_only; does not select a `matrixsumlist` reading and does not
reopen or close `G-YIN-001`/`G-MSL-001`. Does not rule out every
"single-construction" idea -- only the literal, no-transposition,
single-global-pair splice reading tested here; a construction where each
half keeps its own separate treatment and some other operation relates
them remains covered by the existing Phases 271-321/371 body of work, not
reopened by this result.

**Facts affected:** none.
