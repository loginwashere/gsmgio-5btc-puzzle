## Phase 383 -- P32 Family 2 transaction-graph trace: authenticated, no new creator address or route (2026-08-24)

**Question:** the backlog ledger's strongest genuinely-unrun evidence-
acquisition item was P32 Family 2: trace the complete histories of the two
authenticated GSMG addresses, consider only transactions where a seed address
actually signs an input, cluster direct co-inputs, and follow every output one
hop. Does this reveal a new creator-controlled candidate address or route?

**Frozen scope and provenance:** wrote
`tools/gsmg/p32_transaction_graph_audit.py`. Seeds are the prize address
`1GSMG1JC9wtdSwfwApgj2xcmJPAwx7prBe` and halving-storage address
`17ucy1K9ZUAaoY6JVtM932W9jUp5LXfyHa`. Blockstream supplied complete
enumeration; BlockCypher independently matched the full transaction counts;
mempool.space's available newest subsets matched normalized Blockstream
records; exact raw hex for every seed-signed transaction agreed byte-for-byte
across all three sources. Scope stops at direct co-inputs and immediate
outspends -- no recursive heuristic expansion or ownership assertion.

**Result:** 125 prize-address transactions plus 44 halving-address
transactions give 164 unique txids (5 shared). Exactly **2** transactions are
seed-signed, both already-documented prize-address self-spends: the 2020
2.5-BTC halving transfer (`2aa9a4a9...071b13`) and 2024 1.25-BTC transfer
(`88cdb3cd...9b9df3`). Each has 3 inputs, all from the prize address. Every
output goes only to the prize or halving address. The 2020 change output is
spent by the 2024 transaction; the other three outputs remain unspent at the
snapshot. **0 direct co-input candidates, 0 new output addresses, 0 new
authenticated routes.** Both transactions exactly reproduce the pre-existing
`GSMG_PUZZLE.md` mechanics record.

**Disposition:** Family 2 closed negative. Its exact two-transaction raw-byte
cache now removes Family 9's prerequisite, so the bounded
version/nLockTime/sequence/pubkey/sighash/repeated-r fingerprint audit is
unblocked; a wallet-style match remains corroboration only, never ownership
