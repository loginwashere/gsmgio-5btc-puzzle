## Phase 549 — generator fingerprint of FAED and DBBI, seven declared generator models rejected for FAED (2026-09-29)

**Question:** Which declared nine-symbol producers can generate FAED's label-invariant statistics
(sorted proportions, repeat rate, consecutive mutual information, follow, halves and period
chi-squares)?

**Frozen inputs:** seven generators (random-board, VIC-board, hex and random-letter checkerboards;
checkerboard plus VIC chain addition; base-9 English bytes; base-9 random bytes) and two references
(uniform, fitted IID). English from the *Cosmic Duality* book. Mahalanobis omnibus over 2,000
simulations per generator; Holm over the seven, bar 0.01. Locked under the working filenames
`phase548_generator_fingerprint.*` (numbering collision with the parallel Phase 548).

**Method:** controls before the lock. Label invariance held. Size was 0-2% at raw p <= 0.01. Power
was measured at raw p <= 0.01, not at the Holm threshold. A memoryless stream with FAED's counts
was rejected by CB-ENG 100%, CB-HEX 100% and CB-ENG-VIC 71%.

**Result:** FAED rejects all seven (Holm 0.0035 each); fitted IID p 0.434. The checkerboard
rejections are driven mainly by low mutual information. CB-ENG-VIC's sorted-frequency z-scores stay
within -1.36..+1.64. DBBI (reported only): CB-ENG 0.343, CB-HEX 0.421, base-9 encodings <= 0.0085.

**Disposition:** bounded negative for the seven declared models only. Transposing the digit stream
after a checkerboard preserves counts and removes the adjacency the rejections rely on, so
transposed checkerboards over English remain open. Non-rejection identifies neither stream's object
type.

**Facts affected:** None.

**Supersedes/corrects:** None. Extends Phase 412's generic model comparison to concrete mechanisms.

**Reopen condition:** a generator model selected independently of this result; not added boards,
