## Phase 413 -- I0-vs-S0 signal localization and stress-test: driven substantially by one letter, mixed/inconclusive by the frozen criteria (2026-08-25)

**Question:** Phase 412 found `I0` (independent per-stream IID) beats `S0`
(shared IID) on held-out predictive loss, significant after correction
(`0.0566564`, `p_family=0.000599994`). That result says the two streams
have different letter frequencies but says nothing about *where* the
difference lives. This phase is a diagnostic audit of that one already-
executed result -- not a new cipher search -- localizing and stress-
testing it via five preregistered diagnostics.

**Method:** preregistered in two commits (`2591ac6`, `43344ef` -- the
second freezing a bootstrap-seed gap the first left open) before any
diagnostic score was computed.

1. A letter/stream/fold decomposition of the observed contrast into
   macro-loss-unit cells, gated by a hard reconciliation invariant
   (`sum(cells) == macro_loss(S0) - macro_loss(I0)` to `1e-9`).
2. A pooled-label permutation test (661 real characters relabeled into
   91/570 groups, 100,000 trials, frozen `rng.choice(661, size=91,
   replace=False)` call) -- reported as *same-data corroboration*, not
   independent confirmation, since it runs on the same characters
   Phase 412 already used.
3. Nine leave-one-letter-out (LOLO) sensitivities under a **true
   conditional K=8 refit** (the letter is dropped from both training
   and scoring, and `S0`/`I0` are refit over the remaining eight
   symbols with `alpha=0.5`) -- not a K=9 scoring-only mask, because
   letter frequencies are compositional and a scoring-only exclusion
   leaves residual signal about the dropped letter in the other eight
   letters' fitted probabilities. Bonferroni-corrected across all nine
   (`p_family <= 0.005`), each with its own 100,000-trial bootstrap
   null generated from the real, full K=9 shared-`S0` generator and
   then passed through the identical K=8 removal/refit procedure.
4. `b`/`e` reported as ordinary rows of (3), not a privileged test.
5. A per-fold direction check (positive-sign count out of 5).

Three required synthetic fixtures (permutation positive/negative,
LOLO concentration) plus Diagnostic 1's reconciliation invariant all
passed before the real-data diagnostics ran. The LOLO concentration
fixture specifically confirmed the K=8 design choice: a K=9
scoring-only version of the same fixture would not have cleanly
validated detection, since skewing one letter's probability under an
uncorrected 9-symbol normalization distorts the fitted probabilities
of every other letter too.

**Real-data result:**

- Diagnostic 1: the observed contrast decomposes to letter totals (in
  macro-loss units) `b=+0.151725`, `e=+0.043569`, `f=-0.004472`,
  `c=-0.014109`, `a=-0.016635`, `d=-0.017847`, `h=-0.017916`,
  `i=-0.029026`, `g=-0.038633` (sums exactly to `0.0566564`). Letter
  `b` alone contributes **267.8%** of the total observed contrast --
  more than the whole effect -- offset by several negative-contributing
  letters. Fold totals: `[+0.018718, +0.015042, -0.001745, +0.020241,
  +0.004400]` (fold index 2 is the sole negative fold).
- Diagnostic 2: same-data permutation separation is itself highly
  significant (`as_large=1/100000`, `p_raw=1.99998e-05`) --
  corroborates Diagnostic 1's decomposition, not an independent check.
- Diagnostic 3 (K=8 LOLO): **5 of 9** removal analyses remain
  significant (removing `c`, `d`, `e`, `f`, or `h` individually still
  leaves the contrast significant; removing `a`, `b`, `g`, or `i`
  does not). Removing `b` specifically **flips the sign
  negative** (`observed=-0.019710`, `p_family=1.0`) -- the single
  strongest localization signal in the whole diagnostic. `g` and `i`
  miss significance narrowly (`p_family=0.00540`, `0.00828`); `a`
  misses more clearly (`p_family=0.01044`).
- Diagnostic 4: `b`/`e` appear in the table above with no separate
  test -- `b`'s removal is the dominant LOLO effect; `e`'s removal
  (`p_family=0.00324`) still leaves the contrast significant.
- Diagnostic 5: 4 of 5 folds share the overall positive (favors-
  independent) sign; fold 2 (DBBI chars 37-54 / FAED chars 228-341) is
  the exception.

**Branch (per the frozen, exact three-way rule):**
`lolo_significant=5` (`<=6`, the localized bound) but `fold_agree=4`
(`>=4`, the distributed bound) -- the two frozen gates disagree, so
this is **mixed/inconclusive** by the preregistration's own explicit
design, not resolved by picking whichever gate is more flattering.

**Interpretation:** the signal is *not* cleanly distributed across
letters (one letter, `b`, drives more than the entire observed effect
and its removal collapses and reverses the sign) but it is also *not*
cleanly localized to one fold (4 of 5 folds still agree in direction,
and 5 of 9 removal analyses remain significant). Per the
preregistration's own rule, this result does **not**
license plaintext generation, a new DBBI/FAED combinator, or a key
search. Per the registry's asymmetric-test discipline established in
Phase 412, this also does not downgrade or retract Phase 412's own
topology-contrast finding (that comparison and its promotion stand on
their own frozen protocol) -- it specifically qualifies how that
finding should be read: as a result partially, not fully, carried by
distributed structure. T1's "distinct emission profiles" reading is
therefore recorded in the registry as *letter-b-driven with partial
distributed support*, not as fully robust distributed evidence, and not
as a localized artifact either.
